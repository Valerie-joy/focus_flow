FocusFlow

See what holds your attention.

com.scc.focusflow · by CSSDEVS

FocusFlow is an Android study-assistance app. You watch a few short clips while the front camera follows where your eyes go, and it shows you which kinds of content hold your attention. Camera frames are analysed on your device and discarded immediately — no image ever leaves your phone. FocusFlow is not a diagnostic tool and does not screen for ADHD.
