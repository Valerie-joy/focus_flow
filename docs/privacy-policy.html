This Privacy Policy explains how CSSDEVS handles information when you use FocusFlow, an Android study-assistance and self-reflection app.

FocusFlow is not a diagnostic tool. It does not diagnose, screen for, or classify ADHD, and it is not a replacement for professional medical, educational or clinical judgement.

Information handled by FocusFlow

InformationWhere it is handledWhy it is used
Account authentication information and profile information that you provide Managed backend services To create and secure your account, sign you in, maintain your profile, and provide account services.
An optional diagnosis-related document that you choose to upload A private backend storage bucket To store and make available the document as an optional part of your profile. Uploading it is voluntary.
Front-camera frames, facial landmarks, iris points, gaze direction and head orientation used during a session Processed in memory on your device To calculate attention-related observations during a session. Camera frames and per-frame facial or gaze features are not stored or transmitted to the backend.
Derived session metrics, scores, brief self-ratings, questionnaire responses, recommendations and session history Stored on your device only To show attention profiles, study recommendations, prior sessions and generated reports.
PDF reports you choose to export Saved or shared only through the location or app you select To let you retain or share your results. Copies outside FocusFlow are controlled by you and the receiving service.
Standard network connection information Account and content-delivery services To authenticate accounts, operate backend services and stream stimulus video content. Servers may necessarily receive information such as IP address, request time and basic connection details.

Camera processing

FocusFlow uses the front-facing camera through on-device Android camera and computer-vision components. Frames are analysed in memory to estimate gaze and head orientation. The app does not upload camera images, video frames, facial landmarks or other per-frame gaze features. Only derived session results are retained.

How information is used

  • Provide account authentication and profile features.
  • Run guided attention sessions and calculate on-device results.
  • Generate learning-style suggestions, study techniques, content-format recommendations and weekly goals.
  • Store an optional diagnosis-related document when you expressly choose to upload one.
  • Generate a PDF report at your request.
  • Protect, troubleshoot and maintain the service.

Sharing and disclosure

CSSDEVS does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that operate authentication, managed backend storage or video delivery on our behalf, subject to their service terms and safeguards. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or legal rights. FocusFlow does not transmit camera frames or per-frame gaze data to those services.

You control any PDF report that you export or share. When you send a report to another person or service, their privacy practices apply to that copy.

Age requirement

FocusFlow is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and accounts are not knowingly created for anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us at the address below and it will be deleted.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device
Remains until you delete your account, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported reports remain wherever you saved or shared them.
Backend account, profile and optional document
Retained while the account is active. Deleting your account removes them, as described on the deletion page.
Camera frames and per-frame features
Processed temporarily in device memory and never retained by the backend.
Backups and security logs
Backups containing your records are overwritten within 30 days. Security and error logs that may include your account identifier are kept for up to 90 days. Neither is used to rebuild your profile or results after deletion.

View the account and data deletion instructions.

Security

We use access controls and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information handled. All network traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit. Optional diagnosis-related documents are stored in a private backend bucket. No security method is perfect, so please protect your sign-in details and avoid sharing sensitive reports unnecessarily.

Your choices

  • You may decline camera access; camera-based assessment features will not work without it.
  • You may choose not to upload a diagnosis-related document.
  • You decide whether and where to export or share a PDF report.
  • You may delete your account and its data at any time, from inside the app or by email.

International processing

Backend and content-delivery providers may process information in locations other than your own. Where required, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate safeguards for that processing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when FocusFlow's features or data practices change. The latest version is posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will also be communicated through appropriate app or service notices.
