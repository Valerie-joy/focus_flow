Deleting your FocusFlow account

FocusFlow — com.scc.focusflow

This page explains how to have your FocusFlow account and its data deleted, what is removed, and what is kept.

How to request deletion

From inside the app

  1. Open FocusFlow and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

Your account and the data listed below are deleted immediately. You are signed out and returned to the welcome screen.

If anything could not be deleted — for example the app had no internet connection at the time — the app says so before returning you to the welcome screen, and tells you to use the email route below. It never reports success for a deletion that did not finish.

By email

If you cannot sign in, email bethanyivalerie@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with the subject “Delete my FocusFlow account”.

We reply within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm from the registered email address before deleting anything, so that nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

Everything below is erased permanently and cannot be recovered.

DataWhere it was stored
Your sign-in account (email address and password credentials)Supabase Auth
Your profile: full name, email address, age, sexSupabase database and on your device
Attention assessment results: per-category scores, the measurements behind them, session datesOn your device
Self-report questionnaire answersOn your device
Any diagnosis document you chose to uploadSupabase Storage
App settings: theme choice, notification preferencesOn your device

Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored on your device, but it does not delete your account. Use one of the two methods above for that.

What is kept, and for how long

DataRetentionWhy
Server backups containing your recordsUp to 30 days, then overwrittenBackups run on a fixed cycle and cannot be edited individually
Security and error logs that may include your account identifierUp to 90 daysNeeded to investigate abuse and faults

Neither is used to rebuild your profile or your results after deletion.

What was never collected

FocusFlow measures attention using your device’s front camera. Camera frames are analysed on your device and discarded immediately. No photo, video, facial image or facial landmark is ever recorded, uploaded or stored — there is nothing of that kind to delete, because none of it ever left your phone.

Only the derived numbers (for example “eyes on screen 82% of the clip”) are saved.
